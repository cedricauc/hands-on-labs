# Record your learning

For this task, I started by increasing the target speed from 100 to 150. When comparing the final results, the car displayed similar performance regarding best lap, top speed, and damage. Although the car struggled more in corners. The car hit the track edges more often and went off-track more frequently, with more intensive braking.

When I increased both steer gain and target speed, the car entered a corner at a 45 degree angle but managed to straighten itself and remain back on track.

I eventually increased the speed to 200, and the results were much more evident. The car had far less control on the road, started turning in circles at one point, and crashed. Although with a target speed of 200, a brake threshold of 0.9, and traction control disabled, the car mechanically hit the side of the track but managed to finish the lap safely.

Finally, when I lowered the centering gain to 0.1, kept a target speed of 150, and disabled traction control, the car became very unstable. It struggled heavily to correct its position toward the center of the track, moving left and right repeatedly. The car managed to complete the first turn, but on the second lap, the car started moving backward at a speed of 2 and could not finish the race.
